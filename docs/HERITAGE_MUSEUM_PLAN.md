## Expanded museum — continued implementation

The collection now includes all 13 requested starting examples, with source-linked known specifications and explicit gaps rather than invented figures. There are 33 chronological milestones from 1894 to 2026, decade/theme/search controls, a searchable car catalogue, structural diagrams, source-qualified numerical comparisons and fullscreen comparison. Origins now include early road contests, the 1906 ACF Grand Prix, inter-war circuits/championships and wartime interruption.

Four permissively licensed community model candidates (Lotus49, Lotus79, FW14B, F2004) are pinned through public Sketchfab metadata and optional official embeds. No GLBs were downloaded. Search candidates advertising 1983 for MP4/1 and 1978 for MP4/4 were excluded. `tools/review_heritage_models.py` retains metadata/provenance/permission review; it does not import meshes or grant commercial clearance. The named 2026 W17 example reuses the existing local Mercedes model with explicit creator-representation labels. Other historic exhibits use original conceptual diagrams. Models remain unreviewed for historical accuracy and cannot unlock local exploded/X-ray modes.

Eight working educational experiments: wing loads, rotational power, beam stiffness, a spring/mass system, simplified tyre grip, recovery energy bounds, crash work/energy and a Venturi analogy. Seven additional plots are generated from the declared equations; spring movement follows the input. Units, source equations, approximation limits and a large-deflection warning are shown. None is claimed as validated CFD, crash prediction, full tyre dynamics or a real engine map.

`MuseumRaces.tsx` fetches actual archive calendars and links each recorded race into the existing results page. It includes official broadcaster/video archive links and only exposes the known recorded 2024 Silverstone replay. The 1950 season’s seven races were verified live. No synthetic old telemetry or unauthorized footage is added.

`MuseumHistorian.tsx`, `backend/museum.py` and the generated `museum_knowledge.json` provide live cited retrieval with explicit abstention. Optional AI evidence selection is implemented but disabled until a private provider key and model are configured. See MUSEUM_AI_SETUP.md. Public demo calls remain source-only. Mocked API tests do not count as live model-service verification.

Remaining dependencies / limits: authentic full historical CAD and component hierarchies are not available; only authorized creator embeds and diagrams are offered. Profiles are not complete engineering monographs: power, weight, tyre dimensions or designer details not supported by retrieved primary sources remain unverified. The Ferrari technical page required a robot check and was not bypassed; its official heritage PDF supports championship history, not a full technical sheet. Actual AI activation/live verification needs user-owned provider configuration. A full encyclopaedia and ongoing scholarly content expansion are open-ended; this implementation does not claim every event or car in F1 history is covered.
